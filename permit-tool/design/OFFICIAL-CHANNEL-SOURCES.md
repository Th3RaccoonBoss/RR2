# Building Permit Forms

This Building & Inspectional Services Department office is also responsible for the issuance of building, demolition, and moving permits, and the inspection of property.

Please open the forms below with latest version of Internet Explorer. If you are not using Internet Explorer, the Submit button may not work. Please download the applicable forms, fill them out completely and save them to your device.

To submit your information for a building permit, you have two options:

- Email the completed forms along with the required permit documents to permitapplication@joliet.gov, or;
- Drop off the completed forms along with the required permit documents at Joliet City Hall in the large metal permit drop-box, located in the Community Development lobby area.

## Building Permit Information

PERMITS ARE REQUIRED ANYTIME YOU ARE CHANGING ANYTHING STRUCTURAL, PLUMBING OR ELECTRICAL. Stamped architect plans or a letter from a Structural Engineer are required when structural changes are being done.

### Online Permitting Now Available!

The City of Darien is excited to announce the transition to online permitting through our new permitting system, OpenGov. All building permit applications, contractor registrations, payments, and inspection services may now be completed entirely online through Darien’s OpenGov Portal. All plans and other documents required for permit approval may also be submitted electronically through the portal. During our transition, paper applications and in-person drop-offs may require additional processing. We strongly encourage all applications to be submitted through the portal.

If you have any questions or technical issues, Community Development Department staff are happy to assist you by email at mbelmonte@darienil.gov or by phone (630) 353-8115. You can also schedule an appointment and staff can provide an in-person walkthrough for using the system.

 During our transition, we ask for your patience as we work through growing pains. In rare cases, online permits may be temporarily unavailable if technical issues arise. Should that occur, the City will promptly provide notification through the portal.

### Apply Online

→ Apply Online in OpenGov through Darien’s OpenGov Portal → Need Help? Visit the Applying for a Building Permit page contains step by step instructions for submitting applications through the portal.

### Not Sure If You Need A Permit?

Visit our Do I Need A Permit? webpage.

### Contractor Registration

All contractors performing work in City of Darien are required to be registered with the Community Development Department. All contractor’s registrations may now be completed entirely online. - Contractor licenses are applied for and renewed through OpenGov. - All contractor licenses expire at the end of April, unless recently renewed. - General contractor licenses: $60 annual fee. - Roofing & Plumbing contractors: Submit state license only (no fee). - Fence contractors: Not required to be licensed.
