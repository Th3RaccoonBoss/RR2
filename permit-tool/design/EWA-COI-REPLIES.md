# Sophie: Ewa COI reply handling (inactive design)

User correction: Ewa's replies, not general city official correspondence. This module is not imported by the live pilot; it contains no mail/file/database transport. No real reply is ingested, no outgoing message or attachment is filed. Current Joliet request remains a draft, so it cannot satisfy the confirmed-outbound gate.

## Flow
1. Record an authorized, actually sent request with immutable mailbox/message/thread IDs, intended agent recipient, job, city, insured entity and versioned dated requirements. A draft is not sent evidence.
2. A trusted connector verifies the reply's mailbox, actual sender, received time, thread and reply correlation. Display names, subject matches, signatures, forwarded headers and email instructions never authenticate authority. Different sender/new thread goes to review; do not automatically trust a new colleague or changed Reply-To.
3. Treat attachments as untrusted. Only safely inspected readable PDF candidates enter extraction. No remote links, scripts/macros or embedded instructions execute. Encrypted, missing, unreadable or mixed-city documents require review. Preserve original bytes and hash; any normalized/OCR text is evidence, not source authority.
4. Verify extracted fields against the exact current city checklist: certificate holder name/address, named insured and DBA, coverage types and policy identifiers, limits, effective/expiration dates covering the required period, and any required endorsement. A COI is evidence, not an insurance policy or city acceptance. Do not confuse certificate-holder status with additional-insured status; endorsements must be checked separately. Unknown requirements (including presently unverified Joliet limits) block clearance.
5. If Ewa asks a question or requests coverage changes, money, signatures or a new bond, surface the specific decision to Brandon. No silent purchase, assurance, policy alteration or email response. Routine clarification replies require later scoped sending authorization.
6. Plan filing only when destination/private access and authorization are verified. Link to the correct city and job, retaining original and provenance; never overwrite another certificate or silently supersede it. Same-city reuse requires current applicability, not merely matching filename.
7. Suppress duplicate events and identical document hashes for a request. A new hash is a revised document and receives full revalidation. A reserved or uncertain prior filing is reconciled before any retry. Future persistence must use unique event/operation keys, transactional reservation and post-write checksum/read-back; the pure planner is NOT a concurrency-safe worker.

Inactive outputs include identifiers/hash and blockers only, no raw mail, policy number, contacts or attachment contents.
