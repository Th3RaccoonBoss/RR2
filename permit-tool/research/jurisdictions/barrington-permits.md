## INSPECTION SCHEDULING

**Contact (847) 304-3460 to schedule inspections.**

In order to schedule an inspection you will need to provide the following information: building permit number, site address and type of inspection. All inspections must be scheduled at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance. If your call is not answered, please leave your name, contact information, address and permit number and you will receive a call back to schedule your inspection. Your inspection is only confirmed and scheduled when you receive a call back confirming a date and time for your inspection.

## CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION

All contractors and subcontractors associated with your permit must be registered with the Village.  Your permit will not be issued until all contractors have completed the required contractor registration.  Click [HERE](https://vlg-barrington-il.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home "Village of Barrington Online Permit and Contractor Registration Portal") to register online.

#### Submit Permit Application & Required Documentation Online

Apply online through the permit and contractor registration portal [HERE](https://vlg-barrington-il.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home "Village of Barrington Online Permit and Contractor Registration Portal").

#### Permit Application Is Routed for Review

Once all required information and documentation is submitted in the online permit portal, your application will be routed for plan review.  You may receive review comments or requests for additional information during the review process.  Login to the permit portal [HERE](https://vlg-barrington-il.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home "Village of Barrington Online Permit and Contractor Registration Portal") and upload additional information or revised plans.



#### Permit Approval, Payment and Online Issuance

Upon approval of your permit you will receive an email informing you that your permit is approved.  Login to the permit portal [HERE](https://vlg-barrington-il.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home "Village of Barrington Online Permit and Contractor Registration Portal") to pay your permit fee.  Following receipt of your payment, your permit will be issued online.



#### Display Permit

Prior to commencement of any work associated with the permit, **you must print the full set of emailed approved permit documents and permit placard and ensure they are readily available on-site at all times**. The permit placard must be displayed in a visible location on-site.  No inspections will be conducted if the approved permit plans are not readily available on-site.

**ONE PAPER COPY OF ALL PLANS MAY BE REQUIRED.  THE PLAN REVIEWER WILL NOTIFY YOU IF A PAPER COPY OF YOUR PLANS IS REQUIRED.**

- [Apply Online](https://vlg-barrington-il.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home)

- Existing Plat of Survey

### Staking Property Lines

All construction permits for permanent structures and/or improvements including but not limited to additions, detached garages, driveways, sheds, fences, decks, patios, gazebos, porches, pergolas, outdoor fireplaces, swimming pools, sport courts and/or freestanding signs will require the property corner pins of the subject property to be located and clearly staked prior to the pre-pour inspection. It is the responsibility of the property owner to locate and stake the property corner pins. If the property owner is unable to locate the property corner pins, then it is their responsibility to hire a licensed surveyor to do so. If the property corner pins are not located and clearly staked at the time of the inspection, the inspection will not be conducted and a reinspection fee of $50.00 may apply. Work shall not commence until this requirement has been met and the pre-pour inspection has been approved. In the event of any discrepancy, the property owner may be required to hire a licensed surveyor to verify the location of the property corners. The Village does not employ a licensed surveyor and therefore, under state law, cannot locate property corners. The Village recommends that all property owners utilize a professional land surveyor to verify the location of their property lines.

### Building Codes

The Village has adopted the 2018 International Codes which are effective for all submissions received on September 1, 2018 or thereafter. These updates are part of the Village’s goal to maintain a standard of safe and effective building practices that are the most comprehensive to date. Adopting these codes allows Barrington to evolve with the building industry through changing technologies and research data.

Below is a list of the adopted building codes:

- 2017 National Electrical Code
- 2018 International Building Code
- 2018 International Residential Code
- 2018 International Mechanical Code
- 2018 International Fire Code
- 2018 International Fuel Gas Code
- 2018 Property Maintenance Code
- 2018 Swimming Pool and Spa Code
- 2018 International Existing Building Code
- Illinois Energy Conservation Code (2021 IECC)
- Illinois State Plumbing Code
- Illinois Accessibility Code

Along with these building codes, the Village of Barrington has adopted local amendments thereto. These amendments are included to reflect the specific life safety needs of the residents of Barrington as well as specific local conditions. [Building Code Adoption & Local Amendments](https://www.barrington-il.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/OrdinanceNo184014BuildingC.pdf).

One such amendment applies to all new construction and additions which are deeper than the existing structure. Upon submittal of a building permit the applicant is required to provide the Village with a soil boring with the seasonally adjusted water table level data. If the report indicates excavation will occur into the water table, the foundation shall be raised above the water table or the slab and foundation shall be made waterproof.
