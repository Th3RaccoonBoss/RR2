# Frequently Asked Questions

- ### Do I need a permit to replace storm-damaged or deteriorating roofing or siding?









A building permit is not required to replace the most common damaged or deteriorating roofing and siding materials on single-family homes. Even if a permit isn't required, the property owner is still responsible for ensuring the installation meets required building codes and that any roofer hired to do the work is licensed by the State of Illinois.



A permit is not required to install vinyl, aluminum, or cement board siding, or an asphalt composite shingle roof, as long as the existing roof sheathing remains in place.



Installing other exterior finish products, such as brick, thin-set masonry, stone veneer, or roofing materials such as metal, wood, slate, or sealed membranes, requires a permit.

- ### What if my project has multiple features? Do I need multiple permits?









Many projects include multiple features that may require separate permits. For example, a project that includes a pool, patio, and fence will need a permit for each feature. To make the permit process as seamless as possible, applicants should identify the primary feature and apply only for that permit. The primary feature is the project's focus and represents the main scope of what will be built. When submitting the application, include detailed information on all aspects of the project. Based on the information provided, staff will identify and enter permits for the other project features. All project permits, whether created by the applicant or staff, will be available through the portal.

- ### How do I apply for permits?









To apply for a permit, please visit the [City of Naperville Permitting, Plans and Licensing (Civic Access) Portal](https://napervilleil-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService "Permitting, Plans and Licensing Portal"). The portal gathers the information needed to apply, pay for, and manage permits in one convenient place. All permit application documents must conform to the City's [electronic submittal requirements (PDF)](https://www.naperville.il.us/DocumentCenter/View/276).
