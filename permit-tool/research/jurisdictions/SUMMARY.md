# Representative jurisdiction research
Observed 2026-11-12 UTC. NOT frequency-ranked: original 95-job city audit unavailable. No active rules, no legal determination for a specific parcel, no external actions. Source snapshots preserve direct links. Dates are observation dates, not legislation effective dates. Confirm jurisdiction, exact trade/material/structural scope, current registrations and ancillary permits before an actual packet. Unknown does not mean exempt.

## Orland Park
- Roof page explicitly requires permit for all new roofs and reroofs, with/without tear-off. Published fees $95 single-family, multifamily/commercial starting $180. Requires contractor information, cost, homeowner-signed contract, applicant driver's license copy, applicant signature and skylight form where relevant. Final inspection all roofs; insulation inspection flat roofs. State license and current village registration required.
- Siding is expressly a separate permit, not bundled into roof. Siding/gutters/fascia page fee $95 single-family, $90+ multi/commercial; final inspection.
- Contractor-level COI: published code §5-1-6(A)(4), current 2026 S-109, requires licensed contractors COI naming Village as certificate holder, $1m per occurrence general liability and statutory workers compensation. Explicitly includes local roofing licensing. This is registration evidence; check existing registration/COI before requesting duplicate document. No separate per-job COI requirement verified from roof checklist.
- Ordinary surety bond requirement not established in reviewed section; UNKNOWN, not declared exempt. State licensing/bonding is separate from municipal permit bond.
- Driver's-license document is sensitive; preparing a checklist does NOT authorize sending it to portal. Owner authorization would be required before transmitting identity documentation.
Sources: orland-roof.md, orland-siding.md, orland-registration.md.

## Naperville
- Live official Building Permits FAQ, not merely legacy PDF: single-family homes, common damaged/deteriorating materials. No permit for vinyl/aluminum/cement-board siding, or asphalt-composite shingles AS LONG AS EXISTING ROOF SHEATHING REMAINS. Installation still must meet code, roofer state licensed.
- Other finishes (brick, thin-set masonry, stone veneer) and metal/wood/slate/sealed-membrane roofs require permit. Do not extend no-permit rule to sheathing replacement, structural changes, multifamily or commercial.
- COI/municipal contractor registration/bond requirements not established from FAQ; UNKNOWN. No automatic COI request justified.
Source: naperville-faq.md.

## Lombard
- Live official mobile Building Division FAQ explicitly says roofing requires permit, siding does not. Generic no-permit siding statement is not structural-work exemption.
- FAQ's trade-specific documentation list says roofers provide state roofing license. It expressly specifies COI for electricians/excavators/sewer contractors but not roofers. This is not conclusive proof that no other insurance/registration conditions apply.
- Dedicated Contractor Information page returned loading shell on two live attempts. Search excerpt mentioned $300k liability, but not verified in readable body; do NOT activate that amount or apply it to roofers. COI/bond remaining UNKNOWN pending readable current contractor table/code.
Source: lombard-faq.md.

## Village of Barrington
- Official permit submittal page has Roof/Gutters/Siding/Trim category requiring Construction Permit Application plus quote/contract/project/material/product specifications. Historic District may require images/specifications/section details of existing materials. No broad exemption identified; classification/repair edge cases remain review-needed.
- All contractors/subcontractors on permit must register before issuance. Current official Forms page links registration PDF (2023 URL); fee $100 per contractor, registration one year from payment. Roofing line specifically requires state roof license; General Contractor line specifically says COI required. Siding listed separately without a COI annotation.
- Do not assume every roofing/siding subcontractor needs COI on basis of GC line, or treat omission as exemption. Certificate-holder wording/limits/bond absent in form; UNKNOWN. Determine actual contractor classification and existing registration.
- This is Village of Barrington, not Barrington Hills/South/North/Lake Barrington or unincorporated areas sharing postal name.
Sources: barrington-permits.md, barrington-registration.md.
