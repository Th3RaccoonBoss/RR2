## Application Requirements

The following is needed to apply for permit:

- Total cost of work
- Permit fee is $95.00 for Single Family Residential, starts at $90.00 for Multi-Family/Commercial
- **Homeowner's Affidavit** should be completed if the homeowner will be doing any work themselves.

## Inspections Required

A final inspection will be required upon completion of work. Inspection requests must be submitted 24 hours in advance. Please complete an **inspection request**. Final inspection requests are not taken over the phone.
